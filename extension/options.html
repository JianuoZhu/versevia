Translation sends caption text to your selected provider; custom AI and DeepL also receive the neighbouring sentences. Recording sends the explicitly captured tab audio to your speech provider. Providers may charge for requests. No request is made until you connect a provider and enable that feature.

Reading preferences




Finds complete thoughts before translation. Long sentences display at natural clause boundaries. Uses additional provider requests; source captions remain available while processing.




Local data
Preferences and API keys are stored in this browser profile, never synced. Keys are readable by this extension’s pages and background worker, but withheld from YouTube and content scripts. Browser storage is not an encrypted secrets vault. Use a restricted, revocable key; never distribute an extension containing your key.


Translations are cached locally for up to 30 days, capped at 1,000 sentences. Recorded audio is held in memory and discarded after completion or cancellation. Generated subtitles stay in the current page until navigation or reload. There is no analytics or developer-operated server.




Provider and transcription limitations
MyMemory has language-pair, daily-quota and 500-byte limits, and its own text retention policy. LibreTranslate requires a reachable server and may require an API key. Model and language support depend on your provider.
Speech recognition records only the playing tab, at 1×, for 15–60 seconds. It does not download the full video or capture your microphone. Replay the recorded portion after processing. Pause, seek, speed changes, navigation, ads, prolonged buffering and closing the tab cancel capture. Live streams and protected playback are unsupported. Use a transcription model that returns verbose_json with timestamped segments, such as whisper-1; text-only responses cannot produce synchronized subtitles.
